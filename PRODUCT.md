# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primarily recruiters and hiring managers screening for Cloud Engineer and Platform Engineer roles — skimming quickly to judge candidacy, then optionally going deeper. Secondary audience is technical peers and engineers who click into case studies and reports and judge by the depth of the real infrastructure work, not just the pitch. The site has to survive both a 30-second skim and a peer's close read.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio and live project hub for Shlok Shah, combining career narrative, real case studies, and self-hosted running applications into one surface. It exists to do three things at once: earn a reach-out (email, LinkedIn, resume), hold up to a visitor clicking into a live project or case study, and leave the visitor believing this person operates production systems reliably rather than just having built demos. Success is a visitor who explores real work and then makes contact.

## Positioning

A DevOps Engineer (Platform) with 4+ years across financial services (Goldman Sachs) and payments (Razorpay), moving into Cloud and Platform Engineering. The claim is the combination: platform work at scale (Terraform/OPA guardrails, GitOps, DevSecOps in the pipeline, SLO-driven observability, a Backstage developer platform) plus a self-hosted home lab that is actually live today and monitored like production. Most portfolios show one or the other; this one shows both.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: Contact/Email is primary; exploring live projects and case studies is the secondary fallback for visitors not ready to reach out yet.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: "Secure. Reliable. Scalable."
- Belief ladder: this person builds real platforms → and runs them reliably in production → with security and observability built in → contact.
- Proof on hand: the work history, live projects, case studies, the live server-status terminal and the monitoring write-up. No testimonials, press, or logos to work in.

## Brand Personality

Reliable, secure, precise. The explicit visual reference is the Apple mobile website — sleekness and restraint, not decoration, is what should read as competence. Voice is calm and confident rather than salesy; let the real, live, production-grade work speak for itself instead of over-styling the claim.

## Anti-references

The generic AI-portfolio template look: gradient-clipped headline text, glassmorphism used as decoration rather than purpose, identical icon-plus-heading card grids repeated section after section, and a tiny uppercase tracked eyebrow above every section. These read as template scaffolding, which is the opposite of the Apple-mobile-site sleekness this brand is aiming for. The September 2026 refresh removed most of them; see DESIGN.md's refresh log.

## Design Principles

Show, don't tell — proof lives in real live projects and case studies, not in claims about them. Restraint reads as competence — sleekness and precision signal reliability more than decoration does. Every surface reinforces the belief ladder, from real platforms to reliable operations to built-in security and observability to contact. Design for two readers at once — quick-scan hierarchy for recruiters skimming, real depth available on click-through for peers who look closer. Practice what you preach — the portfolio itself runs on the same self-hosted, containerized infrastructure it describes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA: body text and interactive elements meet AA contrast, keyboard navigation works throughout, and every animation has a `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative. No additional known user needs beyond that baseline.
